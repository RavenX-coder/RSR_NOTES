Self Test — Alert System & Detrainment (P15–17)
S1Self Test · Alert System
An amber alert is issued when an incident has occurred which may lead to serious disruption, eg: closure of a station for 10 minutes or longer other than for service capacity or crowd control reasons.
發生事故並可能導致服務嚴重受阻時，會發出黃色警報，例如：受影響車站須關閉達 10 分鐘或以上（不包括基於載客量或人潮控制的原因）。
S2Self Test · Alert System
A red alert is issued when an incident has occurred and will continue or is expected to continue for 20 minutes or longer.
發生的事故會或預計會持續達 20 分鐘或以上，會發出紅色警報。
S3Self Test · SOPM 2B
When a train is stopped where all car doors and platform screen doors or automatic platform gates are not aligned, station staff must: obtain the controller key from the Train Operator; open platform doors or emergency escape doors appropriately; manually isolate and open car doors within the platform only.
當只有部分列車駛進月台，車門與月台門未有對齊，而需要進行清客時，車站員工必須：向列車司機取得控制器匙，並就清客的方法與列車司機進行溝通；按合適情況，打開選定的月台門或緊急逃生門；手動隔離並僅打開可從月台進入的選定車門。
S4Self Test · SOPM 2B
According to SOPM 2B, detrainments are classified as: Between stations - Emergency; Between stations - Non-emergency; At Platforms. Therefore the correct answer is d. i, ii, vi only.
根據 SOPM 2B，相關分類為：兩站之間 - 緊急情況；兩站之間 - 非緊急情況；在月台。因此正確答案為 d. i、ii、vi only。
S5Self Test · SOPM 2B
Particular attention must be paid to: communications, concerning incident control and management, and providing information to passengers; ventilation arrangements; switching on lighting on the detrainment route; assistance to passengers, particularly the elderly, young and the disabled; subsequent restoration of train services.
必須特別注意：發放資訊給乘客；通風安排；亮起隧道燈或泛光燈；協助乘客；以及其後恢復列車服務。
S6Self Test · SOPM 2B
The Traffic Controller, Environmental System Controller and Station Controllers, or Senior Supervisor - Traffic of Light Rail concerned must ensure that their actions are properly coordinated to cater for the individual circumstances of each detrainment.
相關的 行車控制主任、環境系統管理員和值日站長或輕鐵車務執行人員，必須確保能作出適當的協調，配合個別的清客情況。
Part I — Detrainment (P18–20)
Q1SOPM 2B3.2 / 2B3.2.1
The Train Operator of the defective train must: secure the train; when authorised by the Traffic Controller, lower the detrainment ramp or ladders where provided and appropriate.
故障列車的司機必須：固定列車；獲得行車控制主任授權後，若設有清客斜道或清客梯，按情況將其降下，即 將清客斜道或清客梯按情況降下。
Q2SOPM 2B3.2 / 2B3.2.2
When detrainment is to be made onto a track or a trackside or tunnel elevated walkway, the Traffic Controller must: stop all train movements in the affected area; instruct the Station Controllers to switch off impulse fans; advise the Train Operator to detrain passengers when station staff arrive.
若要安排乘客落軌道或軌旁或隧道高架人行道，行車控制主任必須：停止受影響範圍內的所有行車；指示值日站長關掉鼓風扇；在車站員工到達後，通知列車司機清客。
Q3SOPM 2B3.2 / 2B3.2.4
Staff proceeding to a train must: collect a hand lamp and a hand portable radio; standby at locations with potential hazards to warn passengers; guide passengers along the detrainment route to the nearest track access point; assist in restoring the detrainment ramp or ladders after detrainment; inform the Station Controller after a co-ordinated and thorough check to ensure that the track is clear of all persons.
前往有關列車的員工必須：帶備手提燈和無綫電對講機；駐守在有潛在危險的位置，提醒乘客小心；引領乘客沿着清客路綫前往最接近的軌道出入口，並特別留意有分叉點的地方，例如渡綫及位於軌旁的隧道門／前往機房的通道；清客後，協助收回清客斜道或清客梯；在統籌進行的徹底巡查完結，並確定所有人已離開軌道後，通知值日站長。
Q4SOPM 2B3.3.1
When passengers are to be detrained from one train to another on the same track, the Traffic Controller must: arrange for an empty assisting train to couple, if necessary, by a wrong direction movement.
當需要安排故障列車的乘客轉乘同一軌道上的另一輛列車時，行車控制主任必須：安排一輛沒有載客的救援列車與故障列車鈎接，需要時，以反向行車方式進行。
Q5SOPM 2B3.3.2
The Train Operator of the assisting train must: detrain all passengers; confirm with the Traffic Controller the exact location of the stalled train; slide the emergency door and place securely the detrainment plate; inform the Traffic Controller when detrainment is complete.
救援列車的司機必須：讓所有乘客下車；與行車控制主任確定故障列車停頓的準確位置；遵照行車控制主任的指示行車以便進行鈎接；除非故障列車發生電力問題，否則不可利用機械方式鈎接；推開太平門，然後穩固安放清客板；在完成清客後，通知行車控制主任。
Q6SOPM 2B3.4.2
The Traffic Controller must ascertain the train location with the Train Operator by referring to the adit door markings nearest to the leading end of the train.
行車控制主任必須與列車司機確定列車位置，方法是參考最接近列車領前端的隧道門標記，以確定列車的準確位置。
Q7SOPM 2B3.6.3
Detrainment must not commence until both detrainment ramps or ladders, as appropriate, are lowered and staff are in position at track level to guide and assist passengers.
必須先確保兩輛列車的清客斜道或清客梯（按情況）已降下，員工也已在軌道上就位，準備引領和協助乘客，否則不得開始清客。
Q8SOPM 2B4.3.1
If the prevailing conditions require use of both ends simultaneously, the rear end detrainment must be authorised by the Traffic Controller, if possible.
若當時情況需要同時由列車兩端清客，由車尾清客的安排必須盡可能先取得行車控制主任授權。
Q9SOPM 2B4.4
Detrainment to tracks must be authorised by the Traffic Controller and all other train movements must be stopped, except in extreme emergency.
清客至軌道必須獲行車控制主任授權，並停止所有其他行車，極度緊急情況則除外。
Part II — Major Incident & External Authorities (P20–23)
Q10RSR J1.1.1
Major incidents are occurrences which involve: a derailment affecting a running track; a collision rendering any vehicles occupying or obstructing a running track immobile under their own power; closure of a station for more than 20 minutes, other than for crowd control.
涉及以下情況的事件均屬於重大事故：列車出軌並影響正綫上的軌道；列車相撞並導致任何車輛不能靠本身的動力開動而佔用或阻塞正綫上的軌道；以及因故關閉車站 20 分鐘以上（不包括實施人潮控制）。
Q11RSR J1.1.1
On Light Rail, incidents which are treated as traffic accidents are not considered to be major incidents unless service is likely to be curtailed for more than 20 minutes.
在輕鐵上，視作交通意外處理的事故並不屬於重大事故，除非服務可能受阻超過 20 分鐘。
Q12RSR J2.2
When informed by the Chief Controller of a major incident, the Duty Services Manager must decide whether to introduce Incident Control and, if so, arrange for: telecommunications technicians to proceed to the site; assume the responsibilities of Incident Officer; a Station Supervisor or member of Security staff to be appointed as the Incident Officer's Assistant; and the Chief Controller to be informed accordingly.
當值事務經理接獲車務控制中心總主任通知發生重大事故後，須決定是否實施事故控制。若是，應安排：電訊技術員前往現場；擔任事故處理主任的職責；委任一名車站督導員或保安員工擔任事故處理主任的助手；相應地通知車務控制中心總主任。
Q13RSR J2.3.1
On arrival at the nearest station or depot, the Incident Officer must: inform the Chief Controller that local control has been taken, the location of the Incident Control Post, and the means of communication to be used; request an an emergency engineering area if necessary; ascertain whether traction current is switched on and which other electrical equipment and cables are live; proceed to the incident site and confirm attendance by the Incident Engineer.
事故處理主任到達最接近的車站或車廠後，必須：通知車務控制中心總主任已接管控制權、事故控制站的地點和使用的通訊方式；如有需要，要求劃定緊急工程領域；確定牽引電流是否接通，以及其他哪些是帶電的電氣設備和電纜；前往事故現場並確定事故工程師到場。
Q14RSR J2.3.2
The Incident Officer must take complete local control of these arrangements regardless of the presence of any other Corporation officials: detrainment of passengers on trains in the vicinity, in conjunction with the Traffic Controller; co-ordination of train service requirements with engineering work required by the Incident Engineer; co-ordination of requirements for traction current and power supply switching, and for operation of the environmental control system.
儘管有其他公司執行人員在場，事故處理主任必須完全負責此等安排的現場控制：與行車控制主任安排附近的列車進行清客；協調列車服務要求，以配合事故工程師所需的施工工程；協調牽引電流和供電系統開關要求，以及環境控制系統的操作；協調分階段恢復正常服務；與車務控制中心總主任保持密切聯絡，報告最新的現場情況。
Q15RSR J2.3.3
The Incident Officer must consult the Incident Engineer to agree upon a timescale for resumption of through services. A Line Clear must be given before traction current or any power supply is switched on or trains are authorised to run.
事故處理主任須諮詢事故工程師，以協定恢復正常行車服務的時間。接通牽引電流或任何供電系統前或授權列車行駛前，必須完成「綫路出清」程序。
Q16RSR J2.4.1
On arrival at the nearest station or depot, the Incident Engineer must: confirm attendance by and with the Incident Officer; take exclusive responsibility for all technical work; inform the Incident Officer of any facts discovered or decisions taken; agree with the Incident Officer a timescale for resumption of service.
事故工程師到達最接近的車站或車廠後，必須：確定事故處理主任已到場並向其報到；完全負責所有技術工作；通知事故處理主任所發現的實情或所作出的決定；與事故處理主任協定恢復正常服務的時間。
Q17RSR J2.4.2
All engineering teams attending the site or intending to take action of any nature must consult and agree with the Incident Engineer before proceeding.
所有工程小組前往現場或準備採取任何行動前，必須與事故工程師取得共識。
Q18RSR J1.9.1
Prior to restoration of service, the Incident Engineer must issue a Service Restoration Certificate to the Incident Officer.
在恢復服務前，事故工程師必須向事故處理主任簽發「恢復服務證書」。
Q19RSR J3.2.1
Fire Services and Police will establish command posts at or near the incident site which will maintain the closest possible liaison. Fire Services will normally operate from a Mobile Command Unit which may be designated as a Joint Control and Liaison Centre to coordinate rescue and recovery efforts of each participating authority.
消防和警方會在事發現場或附近設立指揮站，盡可能保持最緊密的聯繫。消防通常利用流動指揮車進行指揮，指揮車可能作為指定的聯合控制及聯絡中心，以便協調各參與單位的救援和復原工作。
Q20RSR J3.2.2
When a Joint Control and Liaison Centre is set up, participating authority must appoint an official at the centre to provide liaison between their staff working at the site, the Centre and their own control organisations.
成立聯合控制及聯絡中心後，各參與單位須委派一名人員留守該中心，負責為現場的員工、聯合控制及聯絡中心和本身所屬單位之間提供聯繫。
Q21RSR J3.3.5
The Incident Officer will remain responsible for: safety of passengers and staff; safety of personnel of external agencies; ensuring minimum disruption of train services and station or depot operation.
事故處理主任將繼續負責：乘客和員工的安全；外間機構人員的安全；確保列車服務和車站或車廠運作的受阻程度減至最低。
Q22RSR J3.10.1
The Immigration Department is responsible for co-ordinating contingency measures in case of an incident or cross-boundary train service disruption which affects their operations, including: sending an official to the Joint Operation Centre comprising representatives from the Corporation and various government departments, located at Lo Wu Boundary Control Point or Lok Ma Chau Boundary Control Point; overseeing the implementation of joint contingency measures; co-ordinating evacuation or crowd control measures at Lo Wu or Lok Ma Chau Terminal Building; maintaining liaison with relevant government authorities of Mainland China.
若事故或跨境列車服務受阻會影響入境事務處的運作，則入境事務處負責協調有關應變措施，包括：派遣一名執行人員前往位於羅湖邊境控制站或落馬洲邊境控制站的聯合行動中心（由公司代表和各有關政府部門的代表組成）；監督聯合應變措施的實施；協調羅湖或落馬洲總站大樓的疏散或人潮控制措施；處理與過境程序有關的事項；與國內相關政府機關保持聯絡。
Q23RSR J3.11.1
The Custom and Excise Department is responsible for co-ordinating contingency measures in case of an incident which affects their operations at Lo Wu or Lok Ma Chau Terminal Building.
若事故會影響羅湖或落馬洲總站大樓的香港海關運作，則海關負責協調有關應變措施。
Reporting & Accident Prevention (P23)
Q24RSR A8.1.1
Staff becoming aware of damage to, defects or irregularities in the structure, permanent way, signalling or other equipment or of anything else such as flooding or obstruction likely to affect the safety must: act at once to stop any trains that may be affected; inform the Traffic Controller at once that there is an emergency; state clearly what must be done; wait until the Traffic Controller has made arrangements necessary for safety.
員工如發現結構、永久路軌、訊號或其他設備有損壞、故障或異常情況，或發現水浸、阻塞等任何可能影響安全的情況，必須：立即截停可能受影響的任何列車；立即通知行車控制主任發生緊急情況；清楚說明必須採取甚麼行動；等待行車控制主任作出維持安全所需的安排。
Q25RSR A8.1.2
Unusual incidents and accidents involving severe injury or fatality must be reported immediately to the Traffic Controller.
涉及嚴重受傷或死亡的不尋常事故及意外，必須立即向行車控制主任報告。
Q26RSR A3.2.1
Staff must ensure adequacy of illumination at the worksite, entrance/exit and emergency escape route and maintain these places in good condition and free of obstructions.
員工必須確保工地、出／入口和緊急逃生路綫有足夠的照明，並妥善整理此等地方，保持暢通無阻。
Q27RSR A3.2.1
Staff must ensure that the maximum safe working load of a lifting appliance is not exceeded.
員工必須確保起重機械沒有超越最高安全操作負載。
Q28RSR A3.2.2
Staff must make frequent inspections, particularly of areas to which the public may gain access, to ensure that precautions against accidents are in place, such as illumination, cleanliness, orderliness and freedom from obstruction.
員工必須經常進行巡視，尤其是公眾人士可以進入的範圍，以確保採取了預防措施防範意外，包括有足夠的照明、地方整潔和暢通無阻。
