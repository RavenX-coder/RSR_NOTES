Self Test (Q1–Q5)
Q1RSR G1.1.1
All electrical equipment, apparatus and conductors associated with the power distribution and traction current systems must be regarded as live at all times and dangerous to human life, unless the equipment has been properly isolated and earthed.
所有連接配電及牽引電流系統的電氣設備、裝置和導體，無論任何時候都須視為帶電和會危及性命，已適當隔離和接地的設備則除外。
Q2RSR G1.2.1
No switching of high voltage equipment or 415V main switch boards may be carried out without the consent of the Power System Controller, except in emergency or when local control has been taken.
未得電力系統管理員的同意，不可接通／關斷高壓設備、牽引電壓設備或415伏總開關掣板。在緊急情況下或轉為當地控制則除外。
Q3RSR G1.3.1
A power supply failure which may affect train or station operations, regardless of cause, must be reported to the Power System Controller.
不論成因，發生供電故障並會影響列車或車站運作時，必須向電力系統管理員報告。
Q4RSR G1.5.1
When a switch or circuit breaker shows any sign of arcing or abnormality during or after operation: (i) its condition must be reported to the Power System Controller; (ii) it must be examined by maintenance staff before further operation unless authorised by the Power System Controller.
開關或斷路器在操作時或操作後出現起弧或異常情況時，必須：(i) 向電力系統管理員報告情況；(ii) 由維修員工檢驗後，方可繼續操作，獲電力系統管理員授權則例外。
Q5RSR G1.6.2
Circuit breakers must not be locked in the closed position except when they are used for earthing purpose.
斷路器不得鎖定在閉合位置，除非是作為接地之用。
Part I — Minimum Safe Distance (Q1–Q5)
Q1RSR G1.7.1
For work near or when conducting tests on a high voltage or traction voltage fixed electrical installation, a minimum safe distance must be maintained between any part of a person or any conductive tool being directly handled and exposed live parts.
測試高壓或牽引電壓固定電氣裝置或在其附近施工時，人員身體的任何部分或直接處理的任何導電工具，必須與裝置的帶電外露部分保持最起碼安全距離。
Q2RSR G1.7.1
Minimum Safe Distance: Traction 25kV a.c. 2.75 metres; Traction 1500V d.c. 2 metres.
最起碼安全距離：25千伏交流電牽引電壓為2.75米；1500伏直流電牽引電壓為2米。
Q3RSR G1.7.1
These minimum safe distances do not apply at authorised locations or on approved Engineer's train wagons and vehicles where adequate permanent protection or prominently visible physical separation is provided to prevent persons and equipment from coming into contact with the overhead line equipment.
在獲准的地點或認可的工程列車、貨卡和車輛上，如設有足夠的永久性保護設施或顯眼易見的實物分隔設施，防止有人或設備觸及架空電綫設備，則此等最起碼安全距離並不適用。
Q4RSR G1.7.2
If the minimum safe distance cannot be maintained, work must not start until a Permit-to-Work (High Voltage and Traction Voltage), Sanction-for-Test, or Isolation Record Form, as appropriate, is issued.
若無法保持指定的最起碼安全距離，決不可開始工作，除非已按需要獲簽發工作許可證（高壓或牽引電壓）、試驗許可證或隔離紀錄表。
Q5RSR G1.7.3
Before a safety document is issued, a complete understanding on the switching, isolation and earthing arrangements must be reached between the issuer and the recipient. The issue, transfer and cancellation of a safety document must be made in person except for Isolation Record Form of which the issue and cancellation may be made remotely.
簽發安全文件前，簽發人與收件人之間必須就接通／關斷、隔離和接地安排達至共識。有關人員必須親身進行安全文件的簽發、移交和註銷過程，惟隔離紀錄表的簽發和註銷則可遙距進行。
Part II — Earthing Devices & High Voltage Work (Q6–Q16)
Q6RSR G2.2.1
Before any earthing device is connected to high, low or traction voltage apparatus, it must be verified not to be live by using approved test equipment prior to connection of the device. The test equipment itself must be tested before and after verification.
將接地裝置接駁至高壓、低壓或牽引電壓裝置前，必須使用認可的測試設備，驗證裝置確是不帶電，方可接駁裝置。驗證前後，該測試設備也須接受測試。
Q7RSR G2.4.1, G2.4.2
For work on or near overhead line equipment, approved earthing devices must be applied on each side of the worksite in each isolated section or sub-section. Earthing rods and devices used on overhead line equipment must be placed, removed or operated by staff qualified to do so only.
在架空電綫設備上或附近施工時，須在工地兩端的每一隔電區段或分段架設認可的接地裝置。架空電綫設備所使用的接地棒和接地裝置，只可由具備認可資格的員工裝設、拆除或操作。
Q8RSR G2.5.1
When removing an earthing device, phase ends or overhead line equipment cantilever or contact or feeder wire connections must be removed before earth and connections are removed.
拆除接地裝置時，須先將相位端或架空電綫設備撐架或觸點或饋電綫的接駁拆除，然後才拆除接地端的接駁。
Q9RSR G3.1.2
No person may work on high voltage and traction voltage equipment in any circumstances.
在任何情況下，任何人不得獨自在高壓或牽引電壓設備上施工。
Q10RSR Glossary 詞彙-13
Permit-to-Work (High Voltage and Traction Voltage) is a declaration for work on any earthed high voltage or traction voltage equipment or within the minimum safe distance of such equipment which cannot be avoided from being contacted by any person. Its purpose is to make known exactly what apparatus is isolated from all live conductors, has been discharged, is connected to earth, and is safe to work on.
工作許可證（高壓及牽引電壓）－這是當要在以下情況進行施工工程時，由授權人員（獲電力系統管理員的同意）簽發給主管合格人員的一種聲明表格：在任何已接地的高壓或牽引電壓設備上施工；或在任何已接地的高壓或牽引電壓設備的最起碼安全距離範圍內施工，而這些高壓或牽引電壓設備在施工期間無可避免地會與人、工具或物料有接觸。目的是要確保該名合格人員清楚知道哪些設備已與一切帶電導體隔離、已放電和已接地，可以安全地在該等設備上施工。
Q11RSR G3.3.5
When more than one party is to work under the same isolation and earthing arrangements, each Competent Person in charge must be given an individual Permit-to-Work (High Voltage and Traction Voltage) and each must apply personal padlocks to the appropriate lockout box.
超過一隊工作隊在同一隔離和接地安排下工作時，每隊的主管合格人員必須個別獲發一份工作許可證（高壓及牽引電壓），並用個別的個人專用掛鎖鎖在有關的鎖匙箱。
Q12RSR G3.3.7
The Competent Person in charge must ensure that all members of the working party know which apparatus is live and which is isolated, and do everything possible to ensure safety of the party.
主管合格人員必須：確保工作隊所有隊員知道哪些裝置是帶電，哪些已隔離電源；盡可能確保工作隊的安全。
Q13RSR G3.6.1
If the Competent Person in charge is relieved before work is complete, the Permit-to-Work must be transferred to the person taking charge who must read it aloud to the Authorised Person; both Competent Persons and the Authorised Person must sign the transfer; the Authorised Person must inform the Power System Controller and ensure that personal padlocks are changed.
完工前，若主管合格人員需要換班：須將工作許可證（高壓及牽引電壓）移交接替的人員，而該名接替人員須向授權人員大聲朗讀工作許可證的內容；下班的和接替的合格人員和授權人員須在工作許可證上「移交」一欄簽署；授權人員須通知電力系統管理員並確保已更換個人專用掛鎖。
Q14RSR Glossary 詞彙-21
Sanction-for-Test is a declaration given to a Competent Person in charge of testing high voltage or traction voltage apparatus, making known exactly what apparatus is to be tested and the condition under which testing is to be carried out.
試驗許可證是由授權人員簽發的聲明表格，發給負責主管高壓或牽引電壓設備測試的合格人員，確保該名合格人員清楚知道需要測試哪些設備和進行測試的條件。
Q15RSR G3.7.2
The Authorised Person must sign the cancellation and then remove or arrange to have removed the precautions specified on the Permit-to-Work (High Voltage and Traction Voltage) unless another Permit has been issued under cover of the same isolation and earthing.
授權人員必須在「註銷」一欄簽署，然後撤除或安排撤除工作許可證（高壓及牽引電壓）上指定的保護措施，除非已簽發另一許可證在相同的隔離和接地保護措施下施工。
Q16RSR G4.1.1
When work is to be done in the vicinity of live high voltage and traction voltage equipment and a Permit-to-Work or Sanction-for-Test is not applicable; verbal instructions are not considered sufficient for safety; a Limitation-of-Access (High Voltage and Traction Voltage) must be issued by an Authorised Person to the Competent Person in charge.
在以下情況下，若在帶電高壓及牽引電壓裝置附近施工，必須由授權人員簽發工作範圍限制證（高壓及牽引電壓）給主管合格人員：工作許可證（高壓及牽引電壓）或試驗許可證不適用時；基於安全理由，不可單靠口頭指示。
Part III — Traction Current & Fire Precautions (Q17–Q24)
Q17RSR G5.2.1
When work is to be done on or near mainline or connection tracks which requires isolation and earthing of overhead line equipment: the Engineer's Person-in-Charge must have possession of the track(s) concerned; a Permit-to-Work (High Voltage and Traction Voltage) must be issued.
若在正綫或連接軌道上或附近施工，並需要將架空電綫設備隔離和接地時：工程主管必須將有關軌道劃為工程領域；遵照在高壓設備上施工的規定簽發工作許可證（高壓及牽引電壓）。
Q18RSR G5.3.1
On lines where traction current is switched off during non-traffic hours, when work is to be done which may take place within the minimum safe distance but which does not involve overhead line equipment, the Competent Person in charge must: test and earth the section(s) concerned; inform the Traffic Controller, Station Controller and Yard Master of the exact locations of earthing devices.
在非行車時間內牽引電流有關斷的行車綫上施工，並可能進入最起碼安全距離範圍內但不牽涉架空電綫設備，則主管合格人員必須：測試有關區段並將該區段接地；通知行車控制主任、值日站長和調車場主管接地裝置的準確位置。
Q19RSR G5.3.2
On lines where traction current is not switched off during non-traffic hours, work within the minimum safe distance which does not involve overhead line equipment can be done under an Isolation Record Form. Prior to issue of an Isolation Record Form, the Authorised Person must confirm the overhead line equipment has been isolated and earthed; the Competent Person must reach a complete understanding on the isolation and earthing arrangements and the limit of work stated in the form.
在非行車時間內牽引電流沒有關斷的行車綫上施工，並可能進入最起碼安全距離範圍內但不牽涉架空電綫設備，則可根據隔離紀錄表的安排進行。簽發隔離紀錄表前，授權人員必須與電力系統管理員確認有關架空電綫設備已隔離和接地；主管的合格人員取得隔離紀錄表後，必須就紀錄表上定明的隔離和接地安排，以及施工範圍，與授權人員達至共識。
Q20RSR G5.5.1
No work may be carried out on or near overhead line equipment or equipment mounted on or under electric trains on Workshop and Maintenance Tracks until the overhead line has been isolated and earthed, except for approved tests on live equipment under specific direction of the relevant Supervisor of the Section concerned.
除非已將架空電綫隔離和接地，又或按有關軌段的相關督導員明確指示在帶電設備上進行認可測試，否則，不得在工場軌道及維修軌道上的架空電綫設備上或附近，或在安裝於電動列車上或車底的設備上或附近施工。
Q21RSR A7.1.2
Staff must not pose any fire risks in railway premises.
員工不得在鐵路處所內引發任何火災風險。
Q22RSR A7.2.5
After any fire or after discharge of chemical fire suppressant, the enclosure must be ventilated thoroughly before entry; ventilation must be delayed to ensure no re-ignition is possible; if entry is necessary before thorough ventilation, suitable breathing apparatus must be worn.
火警過後或釋出化學滅火劑後：必須使圍封範圍內的空氣完全流通，方可進入；須待火勢熄滅一段時間後才進行通風程序，確保不會死灰復燃；若需進入空氣未完全流通的範圍，必須戴上適當的呼吸器具。
Q23RSR A7.3.1
Only dry chemical or carbon dioxide extinguishers can be used in the vicinity of live electrical apparatus provided that safety clearances are maintained during handling.
在帶電的電氣裝置附近，只可使用乾粉化學劑或二氧化碳滅火筒，使用時必須保持一定的安全距離。
Q24RSR A7.4.1
Automatic extinguishing equipment protecting any enclosure must remain in automatic control at all times except when necessary for engineering work in accordance with the Rules.
用作保護任何圍封範圍的自動滅火設備必須經常保持在自動控制模式，除非按規則進行施工工程時另有需要。
