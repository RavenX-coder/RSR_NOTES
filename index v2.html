Self Test — Personal Safety & Accident Prevention (P21–22)
S1RSR A3.1
To provide basic guidance in requirements for personal safety and the safety of others. Staff must not: place themselves in danger or put others at risk from any cause; do anything that may distract them from complete attention to and proper performance of duty.
為個人安全及他人安全提供基本指引。員工不得：使自己身處險境或因任何原因令他人承受風險；做任何可能令其分心而未能完全專注及妥善執行職務的事情。
S2RSR A3.1
Staff must: attend pre-work safety briefing before commencement of work; warn against and prevent others from placing themselves at risk; report to their supervisor any occurrence that can or does affect safe and proper working; wear adequate protective clothing and use protective equipment appropriate to the work being carried out.
員工必須：在開始工作前出席工前安全簡介；警惕並阻止他人置身險境；向其主管匯報任何可能或已經影響安全及妥善工作的事件；穿著足夠的防護衣物並使用適合所進行工作的防護裝備。
S3RSR A3.1
Staff must: for work that affects safety, countersign the register before and after the work, or when relieved by other staff, to ensure complete mutual understanding of the safety requirements; use proper working platforms or scaffolding, or be anchored safely to a secure anchorage point when performing a task at a height of two metres or more above the ground and anywhere when safety belt or harness attachment facilities are provided.
員工必須：對於影響安全的工作，在工作前後或由其他員工接替時在登記冊上簽署，以確保對安全規定有完全的共同理解；在離地兩米或以上高度工作時，須使用合適的工作平台或棚架，或安全地繫牢於穩固的錨固點；凡設有安全帶或安全帶扣連裝置的地方，均須使用。
S4RSR A3.2 · 2.2.1
Every possible precaution must be taken to prevent accidents. Staff must: ensure adequacy of illumination at the worksite, entrance/exit and emergency escape route, and maintain these places in good condition and free of obstructions.
必須採取一切可行的預防措施，以防止意外發生。員工必須：確保工地、出入口及緊急逃生路線有足夠照明，並保持這些地方狀況良好及沒有障礙物。
S5RSR A3.2
Staff must make frequent inspections, particularly of areas to which the public may gain access, to ensure that precautions against accidents are in place, such as illumination, cleanliness, orderliness and freedom from obstruction.
員工必須經常巡查，特別是公眾可能進入的地方，以確保已備有防止意外的措施，例如照明、清潔、整齊及沒有障礙物。
Part I — Work On or Near Tracks
Q1RSR F4 Purpose
To state the basic safety requirements for control and management of safe access to tracks, including protection against hazards.
說明控制和管理有關人員進出軌道的基本安全規定，包括針對隱患的保護措施。
Q2RSR F4.1.1
Any person who requires access to a track must give details of the location and nature of the work to the: Traffic Controller or Station Controller for mainline and connection tracks (out of depot limit); Yard Master for depot tracks and connection track (within depot limit).
任何人士如需進入軌道，必須向以下人員提供工作地點和性質的詳細資料：
若屬正綫和連接軌道（車廠界限範圍以外），通知行車控制主任和值日站長；
若屬車廠軌道和連接軌道（車廠界限範圍以內），通知調車場主管。
Q3RSR F4.1.2
Before gaining access, the person in charge of the work must: agree the means for track access, the time when all persons, tools and materials must be clear, protection arrangements, any special instructions concerning safety, and the means of communication; ensure that all members of the working party or parties are familiarised with safety issues such as limits of the worksite, access route, protection arrangements, potential hazards, means for stopping trains, evacuation route, assembly point, etc.
進入軌道範圍前，有關工程的主管人員必須：協定進入軌道的方法，所有人員須攜同所有工具和物料撤離的時間、保護措施、有關安全的任何特別指示，以及通訊方法；作安全簡介及確保工作隊所有成員均了解有關安全事項，包括工地界限、進入軌道的路綫、保護措施、潛在隱患、截停列車的方法、疏散路綫、集合地點等。
Q4RSR F4.1.3
Confirmation that all safety arrangements are understood must be given by: each member of a working party to the Competent Person; each Competent Person to the Engineer's Person-in-charge.
以下人員必須確認明白所有安全措施：每名工作隊成員向合格人員確認；每名合格人員向工程主管確認。
Q5RSR F4.3.1
When a working party may be exposed to danger from moving vehicles or trains, the Competent Person must appoint Lookoutmen to provide protection: located in a suitable position to give warning of an approaching train when it is not less than 600 metres away (a sighting distance of 50 metres is permissible when all trains are operating in Restricted Manual mode under 22 km/h or on Light Rail).
若工作隊的安全可能受行駛的車輛或列車威脅時，合格人員必須在合適位置指派瞭望員提供保護：在正綫，須在有列車駛近至少600米外發出警告（當所有列車均以限制人手模式行駛，時速低於22公里或於輕鐵行駛時，容許最少50米的瞭望距離）。
Q6RSR F4.3.1
A Lookoutman must not carry any other duties.
瞭望員不得執行任何其他職務。
Q7RSR F4.3.2
The Competent Person must: instruct each member of the working party of the position of each Lookoutman; demonstrate how each Lookoutman will give warnings of approaching trains.
合格人員必須向工作隊各成員指示各瞭望員的位置；說明有列車駛近時各瞭望員如何發出警告。
Q8RSR F4.3.3
Each Lookoutman must: occupy the position specified by the Competent Person; watch for and give warning of approaching trains; display a danger handsignal so as to be clearly visible to approaching trains if necessary to stop them; give the working party warnings of approaching trains by sounding one long blast on the horn or whistle and by pointing or directing the working party to the place of safety.
各瞭望員必須：留守在合格人員指派的位置；留心注意有否列車駛近，並於列車駛近時發出警告；在需要示意駛近的列車停下時，向有關列車清楚展示「危險」手號；有列車駛近時，用號角或哨子吹出一聲長響來警告工作隊，並指示工作隊到安全地點。
Q9RSR F4.3.3
Each Lookoutman must: if there is more than one track on which trains may approach, call out the track name or number loudly and clearly; repeat warnings if persons do not move clear or do not acknowledge warnings; indicate to the Train Operator of an approaching train by giving an All Right Handsignal when all persons are in the places of safety.
在不只一條軌道可能有列車駛近的情況下，當有列車駛近時，大聲而清楚地說出該軌道的名稱或編號；在人員沒有完全撤離軌道或沒有確認收到警告的情況下，重複發出警告；在所有人員已前往安全地點後，以「一切妥當」的手號向駛近的列車的司機示意。
Q10RSR F4.3.6
Unless properly relieved a Lookoutman must not: leave the designated position; allow anything to distract attention.
除非獲適當人員接替，否則瞭望員不得：擅離被指派留守的位置；因任何事情而分心。
Q11RSR F4.3.7
If a Lookoutman considers that adequate protection cannot be given owing to reduced visibility of trains or the working party or other reasons, the working party must be warned to stand clear of the track and the Competent Person must be informed.
若瞭望員認為未能清楚看見列車或工作隊，或基於其他原因，而不能給予工作隊足夠保護時，必須警告工作隊離開軌道和通知合格人員。
Part II — Safety Briefings for Engineer’s Possession
Q12RSR-EN F3.2.1
The purpose of the possession meeting with safety briefing is to ensure that all involved with the possession have a common understanding of: The protection plan & responsible persons; the location of all the worksites in the possession; emergency escape route and assembly point.
工程佔用會議連同安全簡介的目的，是確保所有參與工程佔用的人員對以下事項有共同理解：保護計劃及負責人；工程佔用內所有工地的位置；緊急逃生路線及集合地點。
Q13RSR-EN F3.2.1
The meeting provides an opportunity for all those involved to raise any concerns regarding the planning of the works, safety conditions or time to complete the works. Concerns can be discussed, knowledge shared and where necessary a solution that complies with the rules can be determined.
會議讓所有參與人員有機會就工程規劃、安全狀況或完成工程所需時間提出任何關注事項。這些關注事項可以討論、分享相關知識，並在需要時制定符合規則的解決方案。
Q14RSR-EN F3.2.2
The EPIC is responsible for conducting the meeting, and he/she must inform those who cannot attend e.g. Station Controller, Yard Master or Engineer's Train Operator who due to other rule requirements may be located elsewhere at the time of the meeting. This rule applies to CPs who will be responsible for managing the worksite and CPs responsible for applying the protection of possession as instructed by the EPIC.
EPIC負責主持會議，並須通知未能出席的人員，例如值日站長、調車場主管或工程列車司機；由於其他規則要求，他們在會議期間可能身處其他地方。這項規定亦適用於負責管理工地的CP，以及按照EPIC指示負責執行工程佔用保護的CP。
Q15RSR-EN F3.2.2
If practicable, any CP(T) who wants to undertake pedestrian access works should also attend the safety briefing involving train movement to ensure that the activity is understood and managed safely by working parties. However, he/she must not disturb the possession safety briefing of EPIC to prevent confusion. Attendees may attend the safety briefing or through teleconference equipment.
如可行，任何希望進行徒步進入工程的CP(T)亦應出席涉及列車運行的安全簡介，以確保有關活動獲工作隊理解及安全管理。不過，他／她不得干擾EPIC的工程佔用安全簡介，以免造成混淆。與會者可出席安全簡介，或透過電話會議設備參與。
Q16RSR-EN F3.3.1
The EPIC must undertake adequate preparation for the safety briefing. This includes: select a suitable time for the meeting before the start of the works, allowing sufficient time for discussion and wrapping-up of the meeting so that access to the worksite can be made on schedule; identify changes in the Traffic Notice that apply to the works; confirm the protection requirements for possession and manpower to undertake them; identify possible hazards.
EPIC必須為安全簡介作充分準備。這包括：在工程開始前為會議選擇合適時間，預留足夠時間討論及總結會議，以便按時進入工地；找出適用於工程的行車通告變更；確認工程佔用的保護要求及所需人手；找出可能的隱患。
Q17RSR-EN F3.3.1
During the safety briefing the EPIC should record: the name, radio callsigns (where used) and contact phone number of the working parties; location of the worksite; arrangement of point setting including details identifying the set of points; any changes and amendments to the Traffic Notice.
安全簡介期間，EPIC應記錄：工作隊的名稱、無線電呼號（如有使用）及聯絡電話；工地的位置；道岔設定安排，包括識別道岔組合的詳細資料；以及行車通告的任何更改及修訂。
Q18RSR-EN F3.3.3
All other relevant parties should: attend the safety briefing; prepare for the meeting by reviewing all plans, notices and instructions associated with their works; identify concerns or problems with the planned works and suggested solutions for discussion in the meeting.
所有其他相關人士應：出席安全簡介；會前查閱與其工程有關的所有計劃、通告及指示；識別計劃工程中的關注事項或問題，以及在會議中提出討論的建議解決方案。
Q19RSR-EN F3.4.1
CP(P) must attend the safety briefing. If work is arising on a regular basis where a CP(P) cannot attend the safety briefing and start the works in time, the case must be reported so that it can be addressed at the planning stage in the Engineering Works Meeting.
CP(P)必須出席安全簡介。如工作經常發生，而CP(P)無法出席安全簡介並及時開始工程，則必須上報有關情況，以便在工程策劃會議階段處理。
Q20RSR-EN F3.4.2
Under this scenario, the HS shall be given all relevant information even though he cannot attend the official safety briefing. He shall reach a mutual understanding with the EPIC about the placement of protection and arrangements for communication. All forms shall be signed before the HS is going to perform his duties. It will be necessary for the EPIC to inform the HS of any changes arising from the safety briefing that affect the tasks of the HS.
在這種情況下，即使HS不能出席正式安全簡介，也必須獲得所有相關資料。他必須就保護措施的設置及通訊安排與EPIC達成共同理解。HS執行職務前，必須簽妥所有表格。EPIC亦必須通知HS任何在安全簡介後出現並影響HS工作任務的更改。
Q21RSR-EN F3.4.3
All personnel attending the meeting should feel free to ask for clarifications required in order to ensure mutual understanding between all parties on the instructions for the works.
所有出席會議的人員都應放心提出所需的澄清，以確保各方就工程指示達成共同理解。
Q22RSR-EN F3.5.1
Actively participate in the safety briefing, and clarify on the following: Raise concerns with time available for the works, safety issues associated with the works, the location and worksite limits, protection arrangements or any environmental issues.
積極參與安全簡介，並就以下事項作出澄清：提出對工程可用時間的關注、與工程有關的安全問題、位置及工地界限、保護安排或任何環境問題。
Q23RSR-EN F3.5.3
Communicate actively with other parties to ensure sufficient information is collected and mutual understanding is reached. Inform SCs and Engineer’s Train Operators of safety briefing details if they cannot attend.
積極與其他各方溝通，以確保收集到足夠資料並達成共同理解。如SC及工程列車司機未能出席，須向他們傳達安全簡介內容。
Part III — Hot Work
Q24Safety Management of Hot Work D1
Any work processes that generate naked flames, hot splashes and/or sparks which may create a temperature sufficient to ignite combustible materials, flammable gases or liquids subsequently leading to a fire or explosion. Examples include: 1) oxy-acetylene cutting or welding; 2) electric arc cutting or welding; 3) rail grinding/disc cutting; 4) brazing; 5) thermit welding or thermic-lance cutting.
任何會產生明火、熾熱飛濺物及／或火花的工作工序，如所產生的溫度足以點燃可燃物料、易燃氣體或液體，並可能導致火警或爆炸。例子包括：1）氧氣－乙炔切割或焊接；2）電弧切割或焊接；3）路軌打磨／碟片切割；4）釬焊；5）鋁熱焊接或熱槍切割。
Q25Safety Management of Hot Work — Scope
Hot work at locations specially designed for welding and provided with adequate control measures can be exempted from the requirements of PTW(HW) and deployment of FM.
在專為焊接而設並具備足夠控制措施的地點進行熱作，可豁免PTW(HW)的要求及派駐消防監察員。
Q26Safety Management of Hot Work D2
A staff member of MTR or a contractor who has undergone relevant training and been qualified by OTD to ensure that a fire hazard should not occur. When a hot work is in progress, he/she shall: attend the hot work throughout the process regardless of a fire or smoke detection system in operation, and ensure that no potential source of ignition remain before leaving the site.
MTR員工或承辦商人員如已接受相關訓練並獲OTD認可，以確保不會出現火警風險。當熱作進行時，他／她必須：不論火警或煙霧探測系統是否運作，於整個過程中全程在場進行熱作監察；並於離開現場前確保沒有潛在火源。
Q27Safety Management of Hot Work D2
A FM shall not be the responsible person of a hot work concurrently in a work site except for rail grinding. A Fire Marshal has the following roles and responsibilities in a work site identified with fire risks.
除路軌打磨外，消防監察員不得同時兼任同一工地熱作的負責人。消防監察員在存在火警風險的工地上有以下職責。
Q28Safety Management of Hot Work D2-1
General responsibilities of FM: a) Familiarise himself/herself with the evacuation routes and assembly points. b) Conduct briefing to the working party on the guidelines for fire prevention. c) Carry out routine site checks to ensure all fire prevention arrangements are in compliance with the work instructions or method statements.
消防監察員的一般職責：a）熟悉逃生路線及集合地點；b）向工作隊進行簡介，講解防火指引；c）進行定期工地檢查，確保所有防火安排符合工作指示或施工方法書。
Q29Safety Management of Hot Work D2-2
Responsibilities of Fire Marshal (FM) for hot work: a) conduct briefing to the working party on the hazards of the hot work and the preventive measures. b) check the work site before commencement of the hot work to ensure it is clear of loose and combustible material, appropriate portable fire extinguishers are available, and local fire detection/protection systems are isolated where required. c) identify aspects of work requiring special care and supervision. d) ensure the Permit-to-Work System is applied, including: issue a PTW(HW) for the hot work being supervised; cancel the PTW(HW) when satisfied that no source of ignition remains. e) check the hot work area one hour after work completion to ensure that no source of ignition remains.
消防監察員在熱作方面的職責：a）向工作隊進行簡介，講解熱作的危險及預防措施；b）在熱作開始前檢查工地，確保沒有鬆散及可燃物料、備有適當的手提滅火筒，以及在需要時已將本地火警探測／防護系統隔離；c）識別需要特別小心及監督的工作環節；d）確保採用工作許可證制度，包括為所監督的熱作簽發PTW(HW)；在確定沒有火源殘留後註銷PTW(HW)；e）在工程完成一小時後檢查熱作範圍，確保沒有火源殘留。
Q30Safety Management of Hot Work D2-3
Handling non-conformities of fire safety arrangements: Stop work and immediately report to CP and the related parties (i.e. YM/SC), so that CP can inform SH; if a contractor is involved, they shall notify Engineer’s Representative, PC and PRSC for the following: i. investigate the cause of each non-conformity; ii. institute measures to prevent recurrence.
處理消防安全安排不符合要求的情況：停止工作並立即向CP及相關人士（即YM/SC）報告，以便CP通知SH；如涉及承辦商，須通知工程代表、PC及PRSC，以便：i.調查每項不符合事項的原因；ii.採取措施防止再次發生。
Q31Safety Management of Hot Work D2-4
At the outbreak of a fire: a) activate the alarm system by the most expedient means, e.g. fire alarm and call point; b) notify all workers to discharge from the workplace to the designated assembly point and assist in the evacuation; c) use fire extinguishers or other appropriate means to put out the fire if safe to do so; d) immediately notify the related parties (i.e. YM/SC/the Chief Fire Warden of the depot) regarding the incident; e) collect information from responsible person after roll call and ensure all workers are discharged safely; f) immediately report to the related parties for anyone missing.
如發生火警：a）以最迅速可行的方法啟動警報系統，例如火警鐘及手動火警按鈕；b）通知所有工作人員從工作地點疏散至指定集合地點，並協助疏散；c）如情況安全，使用滅火筒或其他適當方法將火撲滅；d）立即通知相關人士（即YM/SC／車廠的總消防隊長）有關事故；e）於點名後向負責人收集資料，確保所有人員已安全疏散；f）如有人失蹤，須立即向相關人士報告失蹤人數。
